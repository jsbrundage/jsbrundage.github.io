# NPCs of the Thornwick Protectorate
## Characters Claude Can Use During Adventures

> **For Claude:** This document is fetched on demand during play sessions. Use these NPCs when the adventure visits a specific town or when you need a named character for an encounter. Maintain their personality, speech patterns, and motivations consistently. Do not reveal entries marked [DM SECRET] to the player — use them to inform NPC behavior.

---

## Saltwind Bay — The Port Town

### Cordelia "Seashell" Brightwater — Tiefling Bard
- **Role:** Street performer and information broker on the docks
- **Personality:** Theatrical, charming, collects unusual shells and weaves them into performances
- **Voice:** Warm and musical, with a slight foreign accent. Deflects personal questions with humor.
- **Useful for:** Selling information, providing rumors, connecting the player to underworld contacts
- **[DM SECRET]:** Spy for a foreign power, gathering intelligence on Thornwick trade routes

### Gareth Ironhold — Human, Retired Ship Captain
- **Role:** Runs a small boat repair service at the harbor
- **Personality:** Gruff, knowledgeable, bitter about something in his past
- **Voice:** Short sentences, nautical metaphors, avoids eye contact when lying
- **Useful for:** Maritime knowledge, hidden coves, smuggling routes
- **[DM SECRET]:** Lost his last ship to pirates, too ashamed to captain again. Blames Captain Tobias Millwright.

### Whisper Nightbreeze — Half-Elf Rogue
- **Role:** Runs a "curio shop" that is actually a fence for stolen goods
- **Personality:** Never speaks above a whisper, claims loud noises hurt her ears. Shrewd and careful.
- **Voice:** Barely audible whisper, precise word choice, long pauses before answering
- **Useful for:** Fencing items, underworld connections, information about criminal activity
- **[DM SECRET]:** Actually working to expose a larger smuggling ring from within. Secret arrangement with Captain Vera Ironwatch.

### Brother Matthias Goldquill — Human Cleric
- **Role:** Scribe at Pelor's shrine, maintains town records
- **Personality:** Meticulous, nervous, deeply principled but terrified of confrontation
- **Voice:** Stammers when nervous, speaks in careful formal language, constantly adjusting his spectacles
- **Useful for:** Historical records, town administration details, potential quest hook
- **[DM SECRET]:** Discovered discrepancies in tax records suggesting corruption. Has evidence but is too scared to act alone.

### "Lucky" Lars Stormwright — Halfling Ranger
- **Role:** Harbor pilot who guides ships through dangerous waters
- **Personality:** Cheerful facade hiding deep anxiety. Constantly touches a silver compass.
- **Voice:** Upbeat, uses gambling metaphors ("I'll bet my compass on that"), nervous laugh
- **Useful for:** Navigation, harbor knowledge, maritime escort
- **[DM SECRET]:** His skills come from a deal with a sea hag. Must sacrifice something precious each month.

### Old Henrik the Gull — Human Ranger
- **Role:** Lighthouse keeper who talks to seabirds
- **Personality:** Eccentric, kind, speaks in sea shanty rhymes when excited
- **Voice:** Singsong cadence, trails off mid-sentence to listen to birds, surprisingly sharp observations
- **Useful for:** Coastal intelligence (via birds), lighthouse network lore, weather prediction
- **[DM SECRET]:** Can actually communicate with animals due to a druidic blessing. The gulls bring real intelligence.

### Granny Weatherworn — Human Druid
- **Role:** Elderly woman who predicts weather with uncanny accuracy
- **Personality:** Cryptic, warm, drops hints about dangers without explaining them
- **Voice:** Grandmotherly, speaks in folksy wisdom, sometimes slips into a more commanding tone
- **Useful for:** Weather prediction, ancient lore hints, emergency healing
- **[DM SECRET]:** Retired powerful druid. Was part of a famous adventuring group. Knows far more than she reveals.

### Captain Vera Ironwatch — Human Fighter
- **Role:** Commander of the Saltwind Bay town guard
- **Personality:** Professional, fair, overworked. Takes threats to her town personally.
- **Voice:** Crisp military cadence, direct questions, no patience for evasion
- **Useful for:** Official quests, guard resources, legal authority, town defense coordination
- **[DM SECRET]:** Secretly works with Whisper to combat organized crime. Frustrated by jurisdictional limits.

### Sage Nerissa Moonwhisper — Half-Elf Wizard
- **Role:** Owner of Mystic Tides Emporium, magical goods and services
- **Personality:** Eccentric, brilliant, easily distracted by interesting magical phenomena
- **Voice:** Rapid speech, jumps between topics, uses arcane terminology then catches herself and simplifies
- **Useful for:** Magical item identification, spell components, arcane lore, quest hooks involving magical anomalies
- **[DM SECRET]:** Studying planar disturbances. Has detected the demon prison failures but doesn't understand the full picture.

### Helena Goldwater — Human Noble
- **Role:** Wealthy merchant family matriarch, runs Goldwater Trading Company
- **Personality:** Sharp, ambitious, genuinely cares about Saltwind Bay's prosperity
- **Voice:** Polished, measured, always calculating value — of goods, people, and information
- **Useful for:** Funding, merchant connections, political influence, economic quests

## Thorndale — The Capital

### Lord Aldric Thornwick — Human Noble
- **Role:** Ruler of the Thornwick Protectorate
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, competent, genuinely cares about his people. Willing to use unconventional methods.
- **Voice:** Calm authority, listens more than he speaks, dry humor, asks probing questions
- **Useful for:** Major quest assignments, political context, resources for serious threats
- **[DM SECRET]:** Willing to fund and authorize covert operations. Knows more about the ancient threats than he reveals publicly.

### Mayor Aldwin Greenfield — Human Paladin
- **Role:** Elected leader of Thorndale, Lord Aldric's cousin
- **Personality:** Earnest, politically ambitious (in a healthy way), wants Thorndale to matter
- **Voice:** Formal public speech, warmer in private, slightly competitive with Lord Aldric
- **Useful for:** Local government quests, town resources, political dynamics

### Brother Aldous — Human Cleric
- **Role:** Scholar-priest researching ancient threats and lighthouse network history
- **Personality:** Bookish, increasingly confident as his research proves valuable. Surprised by his own bravery.
- **Voice:** Precise academic language, excitement when discussing discoveries, self-deprecating about his combat ability
- **Useful for:** Historical research, lighthouse network lore, ancient language translation, religious connections
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has access to temple archives containing pre-Kingdom texts about the lighthouse network and the bindings.

### Innkeeper Martha Goldenheart — Halfling, Lakeside Rest Tavern
- **Role:** Runs the most popular inn in Thorndale
- **Personality:** Warm, efficient, protective of her guests. Former adventurer.
- **Voice:** Brisk and friendly, halfling accent, calls everyone "dear" or "love," steely when threatened
- **Useful for:** Rumors, lodging, connecting with other adventurers, surprisingly good tactical advice
- **[DM SECRET]:** Retired adventurer. The holy symbol she wears belonged to a fallen companion. Still has contacts across the region.

### Merchant Cordelia Fairtrader — Human Rogue
- **Role:** Traveling merchant specializing in rare and unusual goods
- **Personality:** Cheerful, curious, always looking for a deal. Morally flexible about sourcing.
- **Voice:** Enthusiastic saleswoman patter, drops to conspiratorial whisper for "special items"
- **Useful for:** Buying/selling unusual items, information from her travels, trade route knowledge

### Scholar Beatrice Bookworm — Gnome Wizard
- **Role:** Librarian and historian studying Thornwick family genealogy
- **Personality:** Obsessively curious, easily flustered, fiercely protective of her books
- **Voice:** Rapid-fire when excited about a topic, mousy and quiet otherwise, mutters to herself
- **Useful for:** Historical research, genealogy, local history, ancient documents

## Copperfall — The Mining Town

### Foreman Duncan Copperbeard — Dwarf Fighter
- **Role:** Runs mining operations with strict safety standards
- **Personality:** Stern, responsible, deeply worried about things he can't talk about
- **Voice:** Low rumble, speaks in short declarative sentences, dwarven mining expressions
- **Useful for:** Mining knowledge, underground exploration, quest hooks involving the deep tunnels
- **[DM SECRET]:** The mines go deeper than recorded. He knows about the ancient dwarven ruins and the sealed passages. Increasingly concerned about sounds from below.

### Vera "Goldstrike" Mountainborn — Dwarf Cleric
- **Role:** Mine safety inspector and healer
- **Personality:** Devout, practical, uses divine magic matter-of-factly
- **Voice:** Steady and reassuring, invokes Moradin casually, no-nonsense bedside manner
- **Useful for:** Healing, mine safety information, religious connections, sensing precious metals

### Marcus "Tunnelrat" Stone — Human Rogue
- **Role:** Works in the mines but knows every hidden passage in the mountains
- **Personality:** Nervous, resourceful, torn between self-interest and doing the right thing
- **Voice:** Talks fast, looks over his shoulder, drops his voice when sharing information
- **Useful for:** Secret passages, underground navigation, smuggling routes
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has discovered a passage connecting to ancient dwarven ruins. Something watches him when he passes through.

### Artificer Grimjaw Ironforge — Dwarf Wizard
- **Role:** Creates mining equipment and safety devices
- **Personality:** Inventive, slightly mad-scientist energy, passionate about combining magic and engineering
- **Voice:** Excited rambling about technical details, forgets to explain basic concepts, hands always busy
- **Useful for:** Equipment repair/creation, magical item identification, understanding ancient dwarven mechanisms

## Silverford — The Farming Community

### Brewmaster Thorin Goldfoam — Dwarf Cleric
- **Role:** Master brewer whose ales are famous across the Protectorate
- **Personality:** Jovial, generous, takes his craft as seriously as his faith
- **Voice:** Booming laugh, evaluates everything like tasting beer ("that plan has good body but a bitter finish"), dwarven drinking songs
- **Useful for:** Social hub, rumors from travelers, surprisingly good counsel over a pint

### Harvest Lord Edwin Grainwright — Human Druid
- **Role:** Leads the farming cooperative, organizes the harvest festival
- **Personality:** Patient, connected to the land, worries about disrupting natural balances
- **Voice:** Slow and deliberate, agricultural metaphors, speaks about the land as if it's a person
- **Useful for:** Agricultural knowledge, nature lore, druidic connections, festival events
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has a pact with nature spirits ensuring good harvests. Annual rituals required.

### Guard Captain Sarah Brightshield — Human Fighter
- **Role:** Leads Silverford's town guard
- **Personality:** Protective, disciplined, haunted by something in her past
- **Voice:** Military precision, softens around civilians she's protecting, eyes always scanning for threats
- **Useful for:** Security information, militia resources, escort missions, combat training

### Wise Woman Elara Starwhisper — Human Wizard
- **Role:** Elderly hedge wizard serving the farming community
- **Personality:** Cryptic, kind, knows far more than anyone realizes
- **Voice:** Gentle and grandmotherly, occasionally drops references to events from a century ago, then catches herself
- **Useful for:** Magical services, ancient lore, forgotten history
- **[DM SECRET]:** Much older than she appears, sustained by magical means. Has lived through several generations and remembers things no one else does.

## Pinehaven — The Logging Town

### Foreman Bjorn Ironbark — Human Fighter
- **Role:** Leads logging operations, beloved by his workers
- **Personality:** Strong moral compass, torn between economic necessity and environmental conscience
- **Voice:** Loud, confident outdoors voice. Quieter and more conflicted in private.
- **Useful for:** Logging industry knowledge, forest geography, labor force connections
- **[DM SECRET]:** Discovered an ancient grove that shouldn't be logged. Secretly corresponds with druids.

### Talia Swiftaxe — Half-Orc Ranger
- **Role:** Scout who finds new logging sites and protects workers from beasts
- **Personality:** Competent, independent, deeply connected to the forest
- **Voice:** Few words, precise directions, more comfortable with animals than people
- **Useful for:** Forest navigation, beast knowledge, wilderness survival, tracking
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has a romantic relationship with a forest spirit. Knows about the hidden Pinehaven Beacon.

### Brother Oakenheart — Firbolg Druid
- **Role:** Forest guardian who watches over logging operations
- **Personality:** Ancient patience, tests people's character before trusting them, speaks to trees
- **Voice:** Deep, slow, thoughtful. Long silences. Speaks as if translating from a language that has no word for "hurry."
- **Useful for:** Forest lore, druidic magic, nature spirit negotiations, environmental quests
- **[DM SECRET]:** Allows sustainable logging but will act decisively if the balance is threatened. Can communicate with every tree on the island.

### Captain Greybeard Stormwind — Human Fighter
- **Role:** Ferryboat captain between Pinehaven and Saltwind Bay
- **Personality:** Grizzled, reliable, has seen everything and is surprised by nothing
- **Voice:** Weather-beaten rasp, nautical jargon, tells stories that are probably 60% true
- **Useful for:** Ferry passage, maritime knowledge, coastal navigation, old pirate intelligence
- **[DM SECRET]:** Former pirate. Uses his knowledge of pirate tactics to defend trade routes.

## Stormwatch Island — The Weather Station

### Station Master Weatherby Stormcrow — Human Wizard
- **Role:** Senior meteorologist coordinating weather predictions
- **Personality:** Perfectionist, takes weather accuracy personally, brilliant but socially awkward
- **Voice:** Precise technical language, apologizes for every prediction that's even slightly off, passionate when discussing atmospheric phenomena
- **Useful for:** Weather information, magical communication network, celestial observations

### Navigator Luna Starfinder — Half-Elf Ranger
- **Role:** Astronomical observer tracking celestial patterns
- **Personality:** Dreamy, perceptive, notices patterns others miss
- **Voice:** Quiet, poetic, often looks at the sky mid-conversation
- **Useful for:** Navigation, celestial lore, predictions about magical phenomena
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has discovered that certain star alignments affect local magic. Studies ancient texts about celestial magic.

## The Sunward Blight — The Dead Zone

### Scout "Deadlands" Danny — Human Ranger
- **Role:** Guide who helps travelers avoid the worst areas of the Blight
- **Personality:** Grim, practical, quietly compassionate. Watches the Blight expand and feels helpless.
- **Voice:** Flat, matter-of-fact about horrifying things, occasionally reveals deep emotion about the land's destruction
- **Useful for:** Blight navigation, undead behavior patterns, survival in corrupted terrain
- **[DM SECRET]:** Immune to the Blight's effects due to a dying druid's blessing. Seeks a way to cleanse the land.

### Sage Morwyn the Mad — Elf Wizard
- **Role:** Researcher studying the Blight's magical properties from a fortified camp
- **Personality:** Brilliant but unstable. Obsessive about her research. Alternates between manic excitement and deep despair.
- **Voice:** Rapid speech patterns that dissolve into muttering, sudden clarity when discussing magical theory
- **Useful for:** Blight lore, corruption mechanics, magical analysis, dangerous knowledge
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has found ways to harness the corruption for powerful magic. Her research may be making the problem worse.

## Key NPCs Across the Region

### High Priestess Coral — Triton Cleric of Melora
- **Role:** Senior religious figure, maritime magic specialist
- **Personality:** Serene authority, deep connection to the sea, more politically savvy than she appears
- **Voice:** Calm like deep water, formal but warm, occasional aquatic metaphors
- **Useful for:** Maritime blessings, water breathing magic, lighthouse network lore, powerful ally for ocean adventures
- **[DM SECRET]:** Has lived far longer than most realize. Knows about the ancient lighthouse network's true purpose.

### Lyrian Silverleaf — Elf Druid
- **Role:** Representative of the fey interests in the Protectorate's forests
- **Personality:** Alien perspective, values nature above human concerns, but respects those who show the forest respect
- **Voice:** Musical, formal, occasionally confusing human customs with each other
- **Useful for:** Fey negotiations, forest permissions, druidic lore, nature-based quest hooks
