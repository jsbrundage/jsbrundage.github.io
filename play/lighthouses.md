# The Ancient Lighthouse Network
## Secrets of the Beacon System

> **For Claude:** This document is fetched when an adventure involves the lighthouse network. Use it to maintain consistency about lighthouse lore, locations, and mysteries. Reveal information gradually through player discovery — never dump the full picture at once. Entries marked [DM SECRET] should inform your storytelling but not be stated directly to the player.

---

## What People Know

Everyone in the Protectorate knows about lighthouses — they guide ships, they've been there forever, and some have gone dark in recent years. The Saltwind Bay Beacon is the most visible. Sailors trust them. Fishermen navigate by them.

What most people **don't** know is that the lighthouses are connected. That they were built by a civilization older than the current kingdoms. That they serve purposes far beyond navigation. And that their slow failure is a symptom of something much larger going wrong.

---

## The Network

Seven lighthouse sites form a coordinated system along the Thornwick Protectorate coastline and inland. Each contains a fragment of a master control artifact — the **Brass Ring of Eternal Vigilance**. When assembled, the seven fragments create a powerful magical focus that can coordinate all lighthouse functions simultaneously.

### The Network's True Purposes
- **Maritime navigation** — the obvious function
- **Long-distance communication** — the sending stone network relies on lighthouse power
- **Threat detection** — early warning against oceanic and subterranean dangers
- **Binding reinforcement** — the lighthouses strengthen ancient seals and protections
- **[DM SECRET]:** The network was designed to contain and monitor a primordial ocean entity called the Deep Hunger, and to watch for failures in the dwarven demon prison beneath Copperfall

## The Seven Sites

### 1. Saltwind Bay Beacon
**Location:** Lighthouse ruins, 2 miles north of Saltwind Bay
**Status:** Explored. First brass ring fragment recovered.
**What was found:** Partially collapsed lighthouse with intact underground chambers. A spectral keeper guarded the fragment. Communication logs between ancient keepers. A partial map showing other lighthouse locations.
**[DM SECRET]:** This was the primary coastal navigation hub. Its failure is what initially attracted attention to the network.

### 2. Heartland Grove Nexus
**Location:** Oghma's shrine in the Heartland Grove, inland
**Status:** Sacred spring corrupted by demonic influence
**What it does:** This isn't a coastal lighthouse — it's the power source. The sacred spring creates resonant crystals that power the sending stone communication network. It channels energy to all other sites.
**The crisis:** Five quasits have corrupted the druidic circle guarding the site, posing as "purifying spirits." The sending stones across the Protectorate are failing because of this corruption.
**[DM SECRET]:** First sign that the ancient demon prison beneath Copperfall is failing. The quasits escaped through the same crystal resonance network the lighthouses use.

### 3. The Sunken Lighthouse of Deepwatch
**Location:** 12 miles offshore from Saltwind Bay, 40-120 feet underwater
**Status:** Submerged. The island it sat on collapsed 150 years ago.
**What happened:** Duergar excavations from the Underdark weakened the limestone caverns beneath the island. The beacon's magic resonated at the wrong frequency during a storm, triggering catastrophic collapse.
**Current problems:** The damaged beacon causes magnetic interference, leading ships astray. Strange lights attract dangerous marine creatures. A mad duergar named Thorak Irondelve has been trapped in the ruins for 150 years.
**[DM SECRET]:** This lighthouse monitored Underdark threats. Its collapse sealed an Underdark entrance — but the seal is weakening.

### 4. The Pinehaven Beacon
**Location:** Hidden within the old-growth forest on Pinehaven Island
**Status:** Active but dormant. Protected by forest spirits.
**What makes it unique:** This isn't a stone tower — it's a living lighthouse grown from an ancient tree with a crystalline heart. Its root system connects to every tree on the island. Druidic guardians maintain the site.
**Current tensions:** Increased logging operations are getting closer to the beacon tree. Forest spirits are awakening to defend it.
**[DM SECRET]:** Brother Oakenheart knows exactly where it is. Talia Swiftaxe has seen it. The loggers don't know what they're approaching.

### 5. The Copperfall Mountain Signal
**Location:** Highest peak overlooking Copperfall, accessible via mining tunnels
**Status:** Partially active with power fluctuations
**What it does:** Mountain-to-coast communication relay. Built in cooperation with the dwarven Clan Ironbind — the same dwarves who created the demon prison.
**Current crisis:** The demon prison failures are causing power fluctuations in the beacon. The tunnels connecting the lighthouse to the Nexus Crystal facility are becoming unstable. Escaped demons are using these tunnels to reach the surface.
**[DM SECRET]:** This lighthouse has a direct underground connection to the demon prison. Restoring it reveals the full scope of the prison crisis.

### 6. The Silverford River Beacon
**Location:** Silverford, disguised as the ancient grain mill
**Status:** Active but unrecognized. Appears to be a working mill.
**What makes it unique:** The most cleverly hidden lighthouse. It actually grinds grain while housing beacon equipment. Its magic subtly blesses Silverford's agriculture — explaining the region's suspiciously consistent harvests.
**Hidden functions:** Weather monitoring, irrigation enhancement, crop growth blessing, river navigation aid.
**[DM SECRET]:** Sara Goldwheat suspects something unusual about the mill. Wise Woman Elara knows the truth but has kept silent for generations.

### 7. The Stormwatch Weather Station
**Location:** Stormwatch Island, 8 miles southeast of Saltwind Bay
**Status:** The most sophisticated site. Partially functional.
**What it does:** This is the network's command center. Weather control, celestial observation, master coordination for the entire lighthouse system.
**Activation requirement:** Needs brass ring fragments from other lighthouses to reach full functionality.
**[DM SECRET]:** This is the last lighthouse to fully restore. It requires fragments from most of the other sites. When activated, it can coordinate network-wide responses to any regional threat.

### 8. The Thorndale Mirror Lake Beacon
**Location:** Hidden beneath Mirror Lake on the Thornwick family estate
**Status:** Sealed and forgotten by the current generation
**What it does:** Communication with other noble houses, family protection. Contains the Thornwick family's historical records and the final brass ring fragment.
**Access:** Requires Thornwick family bloodline to activate. Underwater entrance through a cave system beneath the lake.
**[DM SECRET]:** The original Thornwick family were lighthouse keepers, not just nobles. This revelation changes the political landscape of the entire Protectorate. Lord Aldric does not know this.

## Discovery Pacing

### How to Reveal the Network

**Don't dump this all at once.** The lighthouse network should be a slow-burn mystery that unfolds across many sessions.

**Phase 1 — A Lighthouse (Levels 1-3)**
The player encounters one or two lighthouse sites. They find a brass ring fragment and a partial map. They learn that the lighthouses are connected but don't understand how or why.

**Phase 2 — A Network (Levels 3-5)**
Multiple sites explored. The connection between lighthouses, sending stones, and regional magic becomes clear. The player understands the network exists but not its full purpose.

**Phase 3 — A Defense System (Levels 5-7)**
The true purpose emerges: the lighthouses aren't just for navigation. They're a coordinated defense against threats from the ocean depths and the Underdark. The player begins to understand the stakes.

**Phase 4 — Restoration (Levels 7+)**
With most fragments collected, the player can attempt to restore the full network. This is a campaign-defining moment with world-changing consequences.

### Key Rule for Claude
When a player discovers lighthouse lore, reveal only what that specific site would tell them. Let them piece together the bigger picture from fragments. The mystery is the reward.

## Integration with Other Storylines

**The Demon Prison:** The lighthouse network and the demon prison use the same ancient crystal resonance system. Failures in one affect the other. The Heartland Grove corruption is where these two storylines first intersect.

**Maritime Threats:** The Deep Hunger — a primordial ocean entity — is what the coastal lighthouses were originally built to contain. As the lighthouses fail, maritime dangers increase.

**Political Implications:** When the Thornwick family's true history as lighthouse keepers is revealed, it redefines their authority. They aren't just nobles — they're guardians with ancient responsibilities.
