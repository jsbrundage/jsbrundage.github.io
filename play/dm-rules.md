# D&D 5e DM Quick Reference
## Rules Claude Needs During Play

> **For Claude:** This is your mechanical reference during sessions. Use it to resolve rules questions accurately rather than relying on training data recall. If a rule isn't covered here, tell the player you'll make a fair ruling in the moment and they can look up the official rule later.

---

## Conditions

### Blinded
- Can't see, auto-fails checks requiring sight
- Attacks against the creature have advantage
- The creature's attacks have disadvantage

### Charmed
- Can't attack the charmer or target them with harmful abilities
- The charmer has advantage on social checks against the creature

### Deafened
- Can't hear, auto-fails checks requiring hearing

### Frightened
- Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while source of fear is in line of sight
- Can't willingly move closer to the source of fear

### Grappled
- Speed becomes 0, can't benefit from bonuses to speed
- Ends if grappler is incapacitated or target is moved out of reach
- Escape: Action, contested Athletics or Acrobatics vs. grappler's Athletics

### Incapacitated
- Can't take actions or reactions

### Invisible
- Impossible to see without magic or special sense (counts as heavily obscured)
- Attacks against the creature have disadvantage
- The creature's attacks have advantage

### Paralyzed
- Incapacitated, can't move or speak
- Auto-fails STR and DEX saves
- Attacks have advantage; melee hits within 5 ft are automatic critical hits

### Petrified
- Transformed to stone, incapacitated, unaware of surroundings
- Auto-fails STR and DEX saves, resistance to all damage
- Immune to poison and disease (existing ones suspended)

### Poisoned
- Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks

### Prone
- Disadvantage on attack rolls
- Melee attacks against it have advantage; ranged attacks have disadvantage
- Must spend half movement to stand up

### Restrained
- Speed becomes 0
- Attacks against it have advantage; its attacks have disadvantage
- Disadvantage on DEX saves

### Stunned
- Incapacitated, can't move, can speak only falteringly
- Auto-fails STR and DEX saves
- Attacks against it have advantage

### Unconscious
- Incapacitated, drops what it's holding, falls prone
- Auto-fails STR and DEX saves
- Attacks have advantage; melee hits within 5 ft are automatic critical hits

### Exhaustion (Levels Stack)
1. Disadvantage on ability checks
2. Speed halved
3. Disadvantage on attacks and saves
4. HP maximum halved
5. Speed reduced to 0
6. Death

Finishing a long rest reduces exhaustion by 1 level (if the creature has food and water).

## Actions in Combat

### Standard Actions
- **Attack:** Make one melee or ranged attack (or more with Extra Attack feature)
- **Cast a Spell:** Cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action
- **Dash:** Gain extra movement equal to your speed for the turn
- **Disengage:** Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn
- **Dodge:** Until your next turn, attacks against you have disadvantage (if you can see the attacker), and you have advantage on DEX saves. Lost if incapacitated or speed drops to 0.
- **Help:** Give an ally advantage on their next ability check or attack roll against a target within 5 ft of you
- **Hide:** Make a Stealth check. If successful, you are unseen (see Invisible benefits)
- **Ready:** Prepare an action to trigger on a specific condition before your next turn. Uses your reaction when triggered. Readied spells require concentration.
- **Search:** Make a Perception or Investigation check
- **Use an Object:** Interact with a second object (first object interaction is free)

### Bonus Actions
- Only if a class feature, spell, or ability specifically grants one
- Only ONE bonus action per turn
- You choose when during your turn to take it (unless timing is specified)
- A spell cast as a bonus action limits your action to cantrips only for that turn

### Reactions
- ONE reaction per round (resets at the start of your turn)
- **Opportunity Attack:** When a hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack. Doesn't trigger from forced movement, teleportation, or Disengage.
- **Readied Action:** Triggered by the condition you specified
- **Shield spell, Counterspell, etc.:** As specified by the ability

### Free Actions (Once Per Turn)
- Draw or sheathe ONE weapon
- Open an unlocked door
- Speak a brief sentence
- Drop an item (doesn't even cost free action)

## Vision and Light

### Light Levels
- **Bright Light:** Normal vision for all creatures
- **Dim Light (Lightly Obscured):** Disadvantage on Perception checks relying on sight. Darkvision treats darkness as dim light.
- **Darkness (Heavily Obscured):** Effectively blinded without darkvision or a light source

### Darkvision
- Can see in dim light as if bright light (within range, usually 60 ft)
- Can see in darkness as if dim light (within range) — but only in shades of grey
- Does NOT let you see in magical darkness

### Light Sources
| Source | Bright | Dim | Duration |
|--------|--------|-----|----------|
| Candle | 5 ft | 10 ft | 1 hour |
| Torch | 20 ft | 40 ft | 1 hour |
| Lantern (hooded) | 30 ft | 60 ft | 6 hours |
| Light cantrip | 20 ft | 40 ft | 1 hour (concentration-free) |
| Daylight spell | 60 ft | 120 ft | 1 hour |

## Resting

### Short Rest (1+ hours)
- Spend Hit Dice to recover HP (roll each die + CON modifier)
- Some class features recharge (Fighter's Second Wind, Warlock spell slots, etc.)
- Can be interrupted by combat or other strenuous activity

### Long Rest (8 hours, max 2 hours on watch)
- Regain all HP
- Regain all spent spell slots
- Regain up to half of total Hit Dice (minimum 1)
- Reduce exhaustion by 1 level (if food/water available)
- Can only benefit from one long rest per 24 hours
- Must have at least 1 HP to take a long rest

## Concentration

### Rules
- Only ONE concentration spell at a time
- Casting another concentration spell ends the current one
- Taking damage requires a CON save: DC = 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher
- Being incapacitated or killed ends concentration
- Environmental interference (DM's call) can require a check

### Common Concentration Spells
Hunter's Mark, Bless, Shield of Faith, Hold Person, Entangle, Faerie Fire, Spiritual Weapon (NOT concentration), Spirit Guardians, Haste, Fly

## Death and Dying

### Dropping to 0 HP
- Fall unconscious and begin making death saving throws
- Stable at 3 successes, dead at 3 failures
- Natural 20 on death save: regain 1 HP and consciousness
- Natural 1 on death save: counts as 2 failures
- Taking damage while at 0 HP: automatic failure (critical hit = 2 failures)
- Massive damage (remaining damage equals or exceeds max HP): instant death

### Stabilization
- Successful Medicine check (DC 10) stabilizes
- Any healing (even 1 HP) stabilizes and restores consciousness
- Spare the Dying cantrip stabilizes
- Stable creature regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours

## Environmental Hazards

### Falling
- 1d6 bludgeoning damage per 10 feet fallen (max 20d6 at 200 ft)
- Land prone

### Suffocation
- Can hold breath for 1 + CON modifier minutes (minimum 30 seconds)
- When out of breath: survive CON modifier rounds (minimum 1)
- Then drop to 0 HP and begin dying

### Drowning
- Same as suffocation, but combat/strenuous activity shortens breath holding

### Extreme Cold (below 0°F)
- DC 10 CON save each hour or gain 1 level of exhaustion
- Cold resistance or cold weather gear = automatic success

### Extreme Heat (above 100°F)
- DC 5 CON save each hour (+1 per subsequent hour) or gain 1 level of exhaustion
- Heavy armor or heavy clothing = disadvantage on the save

### Fire
- Start of turn in fire: 1d10 fire damage (larger fires = more dice)
- Catching fire: 1d10 at start of each turn until action used to extinguish

## Skill Checks — Suggested DCs

| Difficulty | DC | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Very Easy | 5 | Notice an open door |
| Easy | 10 | Climb a rough wall, calm a skittish horse |
| Medium | 15 | Pick a typical lock, track a creature in mud |
| Hard | 20 | Pick a quality lock, track over stone |
| Very Hard | 25 | Pick a masterwork lock, track in rain |
| Nearly Impossible | 30 | Feats of legendary difficulty |

### Passive Checks
- Passive score = 10 + all modifiers (proficiency, ability, etc.)
- Advantage = +5 to passive score
- Disadvantage = -5 to passive score
- Use for Perception (noticing hidden things), Insight (detecting lies), and other ongoing awareness

### When to Call for Rolls
- **Always roll:** When failure has meaningful consequences and success isn't guaranteed
- **Auto-succeed:** When the task is trivially easy for the character or there's no time pressure
- **Auto-fail:** When the task is impossible regardless of skill
- **Don't roll for the same thing twice** in the same situation unless circumstances change

## Spellcasting Quick Reference

### Spell Attack Roll
- d20 + spellcasting ability modifier + proficiency bonus

### Spell Save DC
- 8 + spellcasting ability modifier + proficiency bonus

### Spellcasting Abilities by Class
| Class | Ability |
|-------|---------|
| Cleric | Wisdom |
| Paladin | Charisma |
| Ranger | Wisdom |

### Bonus Action Spell Rule
If you cast a spell as a bonus action, the only other spell you can cast that turn is a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action.

### Ritual Casting
- Takes 10 minutes longer than normal casting time
- Doesn't expend a spell slot
- Must have the ritual tag
- Clerics can ritual cast any prepared spell with the ritual tag
- Rangers cannot ritual cast

## Combat Flow Reminder

1. **Determine surprise** (if applicable)
2. **Roll initiative** (d20 + DEX modifier)
3. **Take turns in initiative order:**
   - Movement (can split before/after action)
   - Action
   - Bonus action (if available)
   - Free interaction (draw weapon, open door)
4. **Reactions** occur on other creatures' turns
5. **Repeat** until combat ends

### Surprise
- Surprised creatures can't move or take actions on their first turn
- They can't take reactions until their first turn ends
- Compare Stealth vs. passive Perception to determine surprise

## Object Interaction and Improvised Rules

### Breaking Objects
| Object | AC | HP |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Glass/ice | 13 | 2-5 |
| Wood/bone | 15 | 10-20 |
| Stone | 17 | 20-40 |
| Iron/steel | 19 | 30-50 |

### Improvised Weapons
- Proficiency only if the object is similar to a weapon you're proficient with
- Damage: 1d4 for most small objects, up to 1d8 for larger improvised weapons
- Range (thrown): 20/60 ft

### Shoving
- Contested Athletics check (attacker) vs. Athletics or Acrobatics (defender)
- Success: push target 5 ft away OR knock them prone
- Target must be no more than one size larger than you
