# The Thornwick Protectorate — World Details
## Expanded Setting Reference for AI Dungeon Masters

> **For Claude:** This document is fetched on demand when you need deeper location details than the world brief in the main /play prompt provides. Use it to create immersive, consistent descriptions of towns, locations, and environments. Sensory details are provided — use 2-3 per scene, not all of them.

---

## Saltwind Bay — The Port Town

### Atmosphere
Salt spray, creaking ship timber, the cry of gulls. The harbor smells of brine, tar, and fresh fish. Cobblestone streets near the water give way to packed dirt roads uphill. The town rises in tiers from the waterfront — docks at the bottom, merchant quarter in the middle, nicer residences and temples on the hill.

### Key Locations

**The Harbor District**
- Busy docks with fishing boats, merchant vessels, and the occasional navy patrol
- Fish market opens at dawn — chaotic, loud, colorful
- Warehouses line the waterfront, some legitimate, some less so
- The Harbormaster's office tracks all arrivals and departures

**The Driftwood Inn** — The most popular tavern for travelers and adventurers
- Built from salvaged shipwreck timber (hence the name)
- Common room with a massive stone fireplace shaped like a ship's prow
- Innkeeper serves a signature drink called "The Undertow" — dark ale with a shot of spiced rum
- Notice board by the door with job postings, wanted posters, and community announcements

**Mystic Tides Emporium** — Nerissa Moonwhisper's magic shop
- Narrow building wedged between a chandler and a rope maker
- Interior is larger than the exterior suggests (minor enchantment)
- Shelves of spell components, scrolls, potions, and curiosities
- A faintly glowing crystal orb sits on the counter — sometimes hums

**Pelor's Shrine** — Modest temple with a golden sun motif
- Brother Matthias maintains the records and the shrine
- Morning services at dawn, open to all
- Small library of religious texts in the back room

**The Goldwater Trading Company** — Largest merchant house
- Stone building with the family crest (a golden wave) above the door
- Helena Goldwater's office on the second floor overlooks the harbor
- Employs a quarter of the dock workers

### Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** Fishing nets drying on racks, gulls wheeling overhead, the lighthouse beacon on the point
- **Sound:** Ship bells, the rhythmic thud of cargo being loaded, hawkers calling prices, distant surf
- **Smell:** Fish, salt, tar, woodsmoke, spiced rum from the taverns
- **Touch:** Sea spray on your face, gritty sand underfoot, rope-rough handrails
- **Taste:** Salt on your lips, fresh oysters at the market, the metallic tang before a storm

## Thorndale — The Administrative Capital

### Atmosphere
Quieter than Saltwind Bay. An inland crossroads town built where three major roads meet. Mirror Lake dominates the landscape — still, reflective, slightly unnatural in its calm. Stone buildings with thatched or slate roofs. Well-maintained streets, a town square with a fountain, and the Thornwick estate on the hill overlooking everything.

### Key Locations

**The Thornwick Estate** — Lord Aldric's residence and seat of government
- Stone manor house, old but well-maintained, with a walled garden
- Guards at the gate but the grounds are open to visitors during business hours
- Lord Aldric holds public audiences on market days

**The Lakeside Rest** — Thorndale's main inn and social hub
- Run by Martha Goldenheart, a retired halfling adventurer
- Warm common room with a fireplace and mismatched furniture
- Best stew in the Protectorate (Martha's secret recipe)
- Adventurers, merchants, and locals mingle here

**Mirror Lake**
- Unnervingly still — almost never has waves even in wind
- Excellent fishing, crystal-clear water
- Local children dare each other to swim to the small island in the center
- Old-timers say the lake "watches" but laugh it off

**The Crossroads Market** — Where the three roads meet
- Market day happens twice a week
- Merchants from all five towns trade here
- The town crier announces news and official proclamations
- Good place to hear rumors and find traveling companions

**Brother Aldous's Study** — At the Temple of Lathander
- Cramped room stuffed with books, scrolls, and maps
- Brother Aldous is usually buried in research about ancient history
- The temple itself is modest but well-loved

### Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** Mirror Lake's glassy surface, the estate on the hill, roads stretching in three directions
- **Sound:** Market-day chatter, church bells at dawn and dusk, the splash of fish jumping in the lake
- **Smell:** Fresh bread from the bakery, lake water (clean, mineral), woodsmoke
- **Touch:** Cool lake breeze, smooth cobblestones in the town square, warm fire at the Lakeside Rest
- **Taste:** Martha's stew, fresh lake trout, ale from Silverford

## Copperfall — The Mining Town

### Atmosphere
Built into a mountain valley. The sound of pickaxes echoes off rock walls. Dust hangs in the air — copper-tinged in the light. Sturdy stone buildings, many dwarven-influenced in architecture. The mine entrances gape like dark mouths in the hillside. A tough, hardworking community that takes pride in what they pull from the earth.

### Key Locations

**The Copper Mines** — The Protectorate's primary mineral resource
- Multiple shaft entrances at various elevations
- Foreman Duncan runs a tight operation with strict safety protocols
- Miners' shifts run dawn to dusk, with a second shift running overnight in some shafts
- The deepest tunnels are officially sealed — "structural instability"

**The Foundry** — Where raw copper is smelted and shaped
- Massive stone building with chimneys constantly smoking
- Heat radiates from the walls even in winter
- Grimjaw Ironforge's workshop is in a side building

**The Miner's Rest** — Copperfall's main tavern
- Underground! Carved into the rock beside the main mine entrance
- Surprisingly comfortable — warm, dry, lit by lanterns and a few magical crystals
- Dwarven ale on tap (stronger than human ale — be warned)
- Arm wrestling competitions every evening

**The Copperbeard Shrine** — Small temple to Moradin
- Carved directly into the mountain face
- Vera Goldstrike leads services and provides healing
- Ancient dwarven runes decorate the walls — older than the current town

### Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** Copper-glinting rock walls, mine cart tracks, lantern light in tunnels, dust motes in sunbeams
- **Sound:** Pickaxes ringing on stone, mine carts rumbling, the echo of voices in tunnels, water dripping
- **Smell:** Mineral dust, hot metal from the foundry, lamp oil, underground dampness
- **Touch:** Rough stone walls, the chill of deep tunnels, gritty dust on everything
- **Taste:** Copper tang in the water, heavy dwarven ale, mineral-flavored everything

## Silverford — The Farming Community

### Atmosphere
Green and golden. Rolling fields of grain and vegetables stretch to the horizon, broken by hedgerows and the silver thread of the river. Peaceful — the kind of peace that makes you wonder what's protecting it. Small town center with a mill, a brewery, a temple, and not much else. But the harvests here are suspiciously good, year after year.

### Key Locations

**Goldfoam Brewery** — Thorin Goldfoam's pride and joy
- Stone building with massive copper vats visible through windows
- Taproom open to visitors, always lively
- The brewing water comes from a "special spring" (Thorin won't say more)

**The River Ford** — The town's namesake crossing
- Shallow enough to wade in summer, bridged in winter
- Fishing spot, swimming hole, and gathering place
- The ferry to downstream farms launches from here

**The Grain Mill** — Center of agricultural commerce
- Ancient stone building that's been in continuous operation for centuries
- The millstones grind with unusual smoothness
- Sara Goldwheat knows every farmer and their business
- **[DM SECRET]:** The mill sits atop the disguised Silverford River Beacon

**The Harvest Hall** — Community gathering space
- Used for festivals, meetings, dances, and the annual harvest feast
- Edwin Grainwright organizes events here

### Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** Golden wheat fields rippling in wind, the silver river, white farmhouses, green hedgerows
- **Sound:** Wind in grain, birdsong, the creak of the mill wheel, distant cattle lowing
- **Smell:** Fresh-cut hay, baking bread, river water, Thorin's brewery malt
- **Touch:** Warm sun, cool river water, rough grain stalks, soft loamy earth
- **Taste:** Fresh vegetables, Goldfoam ale, river-caught fish, warm bread with butter

## Pinehaven — The Logging Town

### Atmosphere
An island town surrounded by massive old-growth forest. The trees here are ancient — cathedral-tall, with trunks wider than houses. The air smells of pine resin and sawdust. The ferry from Saltwind Bay arrives at a wooden dock below the mill. The town is compact and practical, built from the timber it harvests. The forest feels alive. Watchful.

### Key Locations

**The Sawmill** — Pinehaven's economic heart
- Massive operation powered by a diverted stream
- The screech of saws and the crash of falling timber define the soundscape
- Bjorn Ironbark's office overlooks the main cutting floor

**The Timber Dock** — Where processed lumber ships out
- Barges loaded with milled timber depart regularly for Saltwind Bay
- Captain Greybeard's ferry berths here

**The Logger's Lodge** — Pinehaven's only inn
- Rough-hewn timber construction, built to be functional not fancy
- Strong drink, hearty food, loud conversation
- A carved bear guards the front door (loggers rub its nose for luck)

**The Old Growth** — The deep forest beyond the cutting zones
- Trees so large that four people linking arms can't encircle them
- Filtered green light, thick undergrowth, game trails
- Loggers respect certain boundaries — trees marked with a specific knot are never cut
- **[DM SECRET]:** The Pinehaven Beacon is hidden deep in the Old Growth, grown from an ancient tree

### Sensory Palette
- **Sight:** Cathedral-tall trees, sawdust drifting like snow, the mountain's peak above the canopy
- **Sound:** Saws buzzing, axes thudding, trees cracking and falling, birds in the canopy, eerie silence deeper in
- **Smell:** Pine resin (overwhelming), sawdust, woodsmoke, damp earth, wild mushrooms
- **Touch:** Rough bark, sticky sap, soft pine needles underfoot, cool shade even at midday
- **Taste:** Pine-flavored everything (the water, the air, even the ale), wild berries, smoked venison

## The Wilderness Between Towns

### The Forest Roads
- Maintained but narrow, single-wagon width in most places
- Patrol markers every mile — carved wooden posts with the Thornwick crest
- Established camping sites every 15-20 miles with fire rings and rain shelters
- Safe during daylight. Less so after dark.

### Common Wilderness Encounters
- Wolf packs (especially dusk/dawn)
- Bandit ambushes at narrow passes
- Merchant caravans (friendly, may need help)
- Thornwick patrol (3-5 guards, will share news)
- Strange tracks or sounds from deeper in the forest (foreshadowing)

### The Sunward Blight Border
- The transition is gradual at first — trees lose color, grass thins, birds go quiet
- Then suddenly stark: a line where green gives way to grey ash and bleached-white trees
- The silence is oppressive. No birdsong, no insects, no wind in dead branches.
- Undead wander the edges, especially at night
- The deeper you go, the wronger everything feels — gravity seems slightly off, shadows fall in wrong directions

### Sensory Palette for the Blight
- **Sight:** White skeletal trees, grey ash earth, sourceless shadows, occasional movement at the edge of vision
- **Sound:** Absolute silence — then a sound that shouldn't be there. Cracking. Whispering. Your own heartbeat.
- **Smell:** Nothing at first. Then something sweet and wrong, like flowers rotting
- **Touch:** The air feels thick. The ground crunches like old bones. Cold spots with no source.
- **Taste:** Ash. Everything tastes of ash.

## The Stormhaven Gulf — The Western Sea

### Character
Cold, deep, storm-prone. Rich fishing grounds close to shore, dangerous open water beyond. The gulf narrows between the mainland and the outer islands, creating powerful currents and tidal races. Fog rolls in without warning.

### Maritime Features
- **Saltwind Beacon:** Navigation lighthouse on a small rocky island 15 miles northwest of Saltwind Bay
- **Wraithfog Isle:** ~20 miles west, with a small fishing village and Protectorate outpost. Known for thick fogs.
- **Aldric Island:** ~60 miles offshore. Stormwatch weather station on the south end, the remote outpost of Seafoam on the far north.
- **Timberfall Island:** South of Aldric Island. Home to Pinehaven.
- **Dangerous waters:** Submerged rocks, unpredictable currents, and increasingly aggressive sea creatures

### Sensory Palette for Sea Travel
- **Sight:** Grey-green water, whitecaps, distant island silhouettes, gulls marking fishing grounds
- **Sound:** Wind in rigging, waves against the hull, the creak of timber, distant thunder
- **Smell:** Salt, fish, tar, wet rope, the ozonic tang before a storm
- **Touch:** Spray on your face, the deck rolling under your feet, cold wind cutting through cloaks
- **Taste:** Salt. Everything at sea tastes of salt.

## Weather and Seasons

The Protectorate has a Pacific Northwest maritime climate — mild but wet. Heavy rain in autumn and winter, fog in spring, warm (not hot) summers. Snow falls in the mountains but rarely reaches the coastal towns.

### Weather by Season
- **Spring:** Frequent fog, cool rain, wildflowers in the fields. Rivers run high.
- **Summer:** Warm days, cool nights. Clear skies most of the time. Best travel season.
- **Autumn:** Increasing storms, dramatic sunsets, harvest time in Silverford. The Blight seems to pulse.
- **Winter:** Cold rain, occasional heavy storms, short days. Travel between towns is harder. The sea is dangerous.

### Quick Weather Generation
When the player asks about weather or you need atmospheric detail, roll a d6:
1. Clear and pleasant
2. Overcast, cool
3. Light rain or drizzle
4. Heavy rain or fog
5. Storm approaching (wind picking up, dark clouds)
6. Storm in progress (heavy rain, wind, reduced visibility)
